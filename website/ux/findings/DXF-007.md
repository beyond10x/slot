The search plugin pins its box to the right edge on narrow windows, where this site's theme puts the menu button: on a phone the menu was under the box.
Came with DXF-001's fix. Fix (D-02): the box moves left by the button's 40 px and 12 px margin. `ux/narrow.spec.ts` fails without it ("menu button: covered").
