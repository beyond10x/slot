About 36 are recorded by a direct edge; the rest only through a chain (for example `story:sync-sends-the-git-file-list` reading the deny list of `story:sync-holds-back-secrets` through `story:results-come-back-from-a-node`).

  - `story:warm-caches-across-node-lives` adds a provider `image` to `spec/domains/hostconfig.yaml`, while `story:provider-contract-and-node-up-down` needs an image to launch an `aws` machine.
