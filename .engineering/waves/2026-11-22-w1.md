# Wave plan 2026-11-22: twelve stories in two waves

Accepted by `approval-record:wave-2026-11-22-w1` (the operator: "implement all as 1 /wave").
`aep plan artifact waves --kind story --status active` places the twelve stories in nine waves:
every daemon story shares `crates/slot/src/server/mod.rs`, `crates/slot/src/server/tests.rs` and
`docs/design.md`, and most share `AGENTS.md` and `CHANGELOG.md` (collisions per path: tests.rs 36,
AGENTS.md 36, design.md 28, mod.rs 28, CHANGELOG.md 21, how-it-works.md 15, status.rs 15).

This plan runs them as two waves of units, each unit one implementor working its stories in
order, so that stories on the same functions never run at once:

- The coordinator owns `AGENTS.md`, `CHANGELOG.md` and the generated files
  (`ui/src/generated/types.ts`, `website/docs/reference/cli.md`); units do not edit them and the
  coordinator writes them at integration.
- Units that share a file are given disjoint functions in their briefs, and every pair is merged
  dry (`git merge-tree`) before the first unit merges.

## Commits this plan authorises

One or more commits per unit on its branch, the merges of the units into `wave-2026-11-22-w1`,
the coordinator's integration commits (open items, changelog, generated files, store), one pull
request of the integration branch into `main` merged by the bot after its required checks, and the
same for wave 2. Nothing else.
