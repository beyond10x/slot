The timeline draws a 2 px edge on each running bar that switches between black and the bar's
colour every 500 ms, on every frame, whatever the motion preference; the gates fix the clock, so
no frame of the round shows it. Red is the colour of a failed exit, of an error and of the "over
target" line, and also of every "● live" button, the pane's "● live" and the pool line of the host
chart, so a calm page already carries the alert colour 14 times in the crowded scenario.

One-line fix: no blink (a steady edge), and a neutral colour for "live"; keep red for failure.
