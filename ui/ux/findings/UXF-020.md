Queue, Running and Finished are the objects of 3 of the 7 tasks, and of every question an agent
asks about its build. The first screen holds the host chart, two small charts, the Sessions table
and the top of the timeline instead, at every size from 960x1080 to 1920x1080. Each job task
starts with a scroll; in ux/rounds/R-11/shots/queue-position@1366x768-loaded.png the only trace of
job 79 is "queue 6" in the header.

One-line fix: put the job tables above the host panel, or fold the host panel to one row; this is a
layout change and needs a decision record.

Fix (R-11, decision in ux/rounds/R-11/decision.md): the Queue, Running and Finished tables come directly
under the header; the host panel with the Sessions table, the timeline and the cores chart follow.
