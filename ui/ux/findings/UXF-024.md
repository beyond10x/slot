UXF-006 closed a cut session title by adding a `title`. A `title` shows after about a second of
hover and never for the keyboard or a touch screen, and none of these elements takes focus. In
call-mode the card's answer (the 25 GB threshold) is one of them.

One-line fix: show a wait reason as a second line of the row, and put each explanation behind a
focusable "?" that opens on click, Enter and hover.

Fix (polish after R-11): a wait reason is a line of text under its Queue row and in the output pane; the
source of the wait numbers is the sentence beside the Queue heading; the header has a "?" control
(pointer, Enter, Escape) that explains each of its items; the machine line is a sentence in the header
and a paragraph in the Host section; the call mark is a sentence. The output pane shows the command
uncut, the working directory and the session with its full id. Titles that remain repeat text that is
on the page or in the pane of the job, or describe a control. One exception: the Sessions table shows 8
digits of a session id, and the full id is text only in the pane of one of that session's jobs.
