hover and never for the keyboard or a touch screen, and none of these elements takes focus. In
call-mode the card's answer (the 25 GB threshold) is one of them.

One-line fix: show a wait reason as a second line of the row, and put each explanation behind a
focusable "?" that opens on click, Enter and hover.
