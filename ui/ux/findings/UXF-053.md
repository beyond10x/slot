# UXF-053: The cluster page breaks below 1100 px

- id: UXF-053
- title: The cluster page breaks below 1100 px
- class: layout
- severity: S2
- metric: gates.problems (cluster scenarios at 800x600 and 1024x768, gating on the cluster page since this finding)
- status: closed
- found_by: an independent review of the live view at 800 px
- evidence_recording: none (hermetic replay cluster-local, cluster-remote, cluster-old-daemon and cluster-queue-empty at 800x600 and 1024x768)
- evidence_screenshot: none kept in the repository
- evidence_lines: at 800 px the page scrolled sideways (scrollWidth 1084), "slot cluster" lay under "running 2 jobs" and "cloud cost" under the theme button, node names were cut to 3 letters and the right half of each node card was off screen; the gate measured 60 to 284 px of sideways scroll and colour-contrast failures where texts overlapped
- regression_asset: ux/findings.spec.ts "UXF-053 @ 800x600: the start page stacks; nothing in its header overlaps, every node name and card is shown in full" (and the same at 1024x768), and ux/gates.spec.ts at the sizes of `CLUSTER_GATING` (ux/viewports.ts)
- scenarios: cluster-local, cluster-remote, cluster-old-daemon, cluster-queue-empty
- cost: M

The page was laid out for one fixed row of endpoint, lanes and node cards, and for one header line.

Fix: below 1100 px the page stacks and scrolls down. The header numbers take a row of their own;
the endpoint stands above the node cards, without the lanes between them; a node card takes the
height it needs, its head may wrap, and memory, disk and checkouts go to a second row; the job list
shows ten rows without the tool and wait columns. The layout gates hold the cluster scenarios at
800x600 and 1024x768 on every merge (8 more layout tests, about 2.6 s each, 4 workers).
