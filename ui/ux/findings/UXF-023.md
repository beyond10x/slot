# UXF-023: The sticky header takes 15 to 27 % of the screen and grows a row with a status line

- id: UXF-023
- title: The sticky header takes 15 to 27 % of the screen and grows a row with a status line
- class: layout
- severity: S3
- metric: layout_shift.total and reading_load.max (machine-headroom: 229 words against 202)
- status: open
- found_by: heuristic review, R-11 (heuristic 4, stable layout)
- evidence_recording: none (hermetic replays video-call-old-daemon and machine-headroom)
- evidence_screenshot: ux/rounds/R-11/shots/review-headroom-40-old-daemon.png
- evidence_lines: header height 118 px at 1366x768 and 157 px at 1280x720 in 8 scenarios, 196 px in machine-headroom at both (ux/rounds/R-11/shots/machine-headroom@1366x768-loaded.png), 214 px at 960x1080; the refused headroom change adds a row: 118 to 155 px at 1366x768, 157 to 194 px at 1280x720, and the page below moves 37 px
- regression_asset: none yet
- scenarios: 11 of 11 for the size; 2 of 11 for the growth (video-call-old-daemon, machine-headroom)
- cost: M

The header wraps to three rows at 1366 px and four at 1280 px, and it is sticky, so that share of
the screen is never available to the tables. Each status line (the error of a refused change, the
machine line, the call mark, the upgrade mark) is one more flex item: when one appears the header
re-wraps and everything below jumps under the pointer.

One-line fix: give status lines a reserved row of fixed height, and move the rarely read numbers
(PSI, recommendation, legend) out of the sticky part.
