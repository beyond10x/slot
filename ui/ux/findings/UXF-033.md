# UXF-033: The Queue cuts commands beside a column that is empty or repeats the reason

- id: UXF-033
- title: The Queue cuts commands beside a column that is empty or repeats the reason
- class: layout
- severity: S3
- metric: commands cut in the Queue of crowded at 1366x768 and 1280x720
- status: closed
- found_by: review of the screenshots after the polish that followed R-11
- evidence_recording: none (hermetic replays crowded and call-mode at 1366x768)
- evidence_screenshot: none kept in the repository
- evidence_lines: crowded: the command column of the Queue is 396 px at 1366x768 and 376 px at 1280x720, and 3 of its commands are cut ("npx playwright test --workers=2 e2e/checkout.spe…"), while the 292 px right of "wait" are empty in every row; call-mode: "disk space" stands in the column "waits for" and again in the line under the row ("waits for disk space: …")
- regression_asset: ux/findings.spec.ts "UXF-033 crowded @ 1366x768: the Queue has no column that repeats the reason, and no command of the queue is cut" (and @ 1280x720), "UXF-033 crowded @ 1920x1080: where every command fits, slots and wait of the Queue stand under those of Running" and "UXF-033 call-mode: the reason of a wait is said once per job, as the line under its row"
- scenarios: every scenario with a queued job; the cut commands in crowded and call-mode
- cost: S

The Queue shared the grid of Running and Finished to the last column. A queued job has no run
time, cores or memory, so those columns held one word ("disk space", "memory") in the rare
row and nothing in the others, and the word repeated the line under the row.

One-line fix: no "waits for" column; the command takes the room.

Fix (second polish pass after R-11): below 1800 px of main column the Queue ends with "wait" and
the command is 292 px wider (688 px at 1366x768, 668 px at 1280x720; no command of crowded or
call-mode is cut). Its slots and wait then stand at the right edge, not under those of Running
and Finished: the two could not both be kept at these widths. From 1800 px (command 612 px at
1920x1080, none of crowded cut), four empty columns keep slots and wait in line with the tables below.
The reason of a wait is the line under the row and a sentence in the output pane.
