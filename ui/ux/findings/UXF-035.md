# UXF-035: A timeline bar cuts its label in the middle of a letter

- id: UXF-035
- title: A timeline bar cuts its label in the middle of a letter
- class: layout
- severity: S3
- metric: none (a canvas drawing; the fitting rule is a unit test)
- status: closed
- found_by: review of the screenshots after the polish that followed R-11
- evidence_recording: none (hermetic replay busy-failed at 1366x768)
- evidence_screenshot: none kept in the repository
- evidence_lines: bars of the timeline end their labels at the bar's edge with half a letter: "parser: cargo clippy --workspac", "Repository unknown: cargo bui"
- regression_asset: src/lib/fit.test.ts (four tests of `fitLabel`)
- scenarios: every scenario with a started job wider than its label (seen in busy-failed)
- cost: S

A label longer than its bar was drawn whole and clipped at the bar's edge, so the last letter was
cut wherever the bar ended, and a bar wider than 60 px always got a label, however little of it
showed.

One-line fix: measure the label, end a cut one with "…" inside the bar, and draw none when fewer
than 4 characters fit.

Fix (second polish pass after R-11): `fitLabel` (src/lib/fit.ts) returns the label whole, or cut
with "…" so that it fits the bar less 4 px on the left and 6 px on the right (the right clears the
edge mark of a running or failed bar), or nothing when fewer than 4 characters fit beside the
ellipsis. The chart keeps the fitted text per label and width, so a frame measures only bars whose
width changed.
