# UXF-026: Reaching a job by keyboard walks every control above it: 26 tabs to job 42

- id: UXF-026
- title: Reaching a job by keyboard walks every control above it: 26 tabs to job 42
- class: affordance
- severity: S3
- metric: screen_only.keyboard_tabs (busy-failed: 26, at every size)
- status: open
- found_by: heuristic review, R-11 (heuristic 9, keyboard and pointer parity)
- evidence_recording: none (hermetic replay busy-failed at 1366x768, Tab from the top of the page)
- evidence_screenshot: ux/rounds/R-11/shots/review-tab-26.png
- evidence_lines: tab stops 1 to 26: slider, three presets, window select, 2 queue jobs, then three stops per running job ("show units of job n", "output of job n", "● live") for 6 jobs, then "output of job 42"; ux/out/bot/busy-failed@1366x768-keyboard.json keyboard_tabs 26 against 2 pointer actions
- regression_asset: none yet
- scenarios: 3 of 11 (busy-failed, pool-share, crowded)
- cost: S

Every running job costs three tab stops, two of which ("output of job n" and "● live") do the same
thing, and there is no way to jump to a table. The newest finished job, the one a failure report
is about, comes after all of them. The timeline's "click a bar for its output" has no keyboard
form at all; the tables cover it.

One-line fix: take "● live" out of the tab order (it stays clickable) and add a skip link per
table, or order Finished before Running in the focus order.

Improved, not fixed (polish after R-11): the "live" button is out of the tab order (it stays a pointer
target), so a running job costs two stops, not three: "output of job 42" is tab stop 22 in busy-failed
(26 before), the first finished job of crowded stop 46. No skip link per table was added and the focus
order is unchanged, so the newest finished job still comes after every running one.
