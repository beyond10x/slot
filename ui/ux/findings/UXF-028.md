The card asks whether slot is holding builds back and what uses the rest. The line holds the
answer as "pool quota 2.0 (floor)" and a list of systemd unit names; the screen-only user passes
by finding the words "over target". "PSI", "(observe)", "(floor)" and "pool quota" are not words
the user would say, and their meaning is in hover titles (UXF-024).

One-line fix: lead with the sentence ("builds are held to 2 of 20 CPUs: other programs use 9.1")
and move the numbers behind it.
