# UXF-032: With the job tables first, the page moves more while it loads

- id: UXF-032
- title: With the job tables first, the page moves more while it loads
- class: layout
- severity: S3
- metric: layout_shift.total of crowded and busy-failed at 1366x768
- status: closed
- found_by: round ratchet, R-11 (measured after the change set)
- evidence_recording: none (hermetic replays crowded and busy-failed at 1366x768)
- evidence_screenshot: ux/rounds/R-11/shots/crowded@1366x768-loaded.png
- evidence_lines: layout_shift.total before and after the R-11 change set: crowded 0.044 and 0.089, busy-failed 0.035 and 0.049; idle 0.035 and 0.035, upgrade-pending 0.088 and 0.088
- regression_asset: ux/findings.spec.ts "UXF-032 crowded: the page does not move while it loads" and "UXF-032 busy-failed: the page does not move while it loads"
- scenarios: 2 of 11
- cost: M

The change set of R-11 put the Queue, Running and Finished tables directly under the header
(UXF-020). The tables are empty until the first state arrives and then take their rows, so
everything below them moves once. Before, the host panel stood there, and its height does not
depend on the number of jobs. The shift grows with the number of rows: crowded lists the most
jobs and doubled, idle lists none and did not change.

The baseline of both keys was raised under decision `R-11-job-tables-first`
(`ux/rounds/R-11/decision.md`).

One-line fix: render the page only after the first state has arrived, or reserve the height of
the tables from the job counts of the header line.

Fix (polish after R-11): the header keeps its height from the first paint (its numbers hold their room
while the first state loads), so the layout shift from the first paint to the loaded page is at most
0.01 in crowded and in busy-failed at 1366x768 (the test). Over the whole scenario with its steps the
shot run measured 0 for crowded (0.089 before) and 0.016 for busy-failed (0.049 before); what is left
there happens after the load, during the steps.
