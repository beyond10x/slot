# UXF-025: The output of a queued job is an empty pane marked "● live"

- id: UXF-025
- title: The output of a queued job is an empty pane marked "● live"
- class: cue
- severity: S3
- metric: none yet (a screen-only "see" task on a queued job's pane)
- status: closed
- found_by: heuristic review, R-11 (heuristic 2, state in words)
- evidence_recording: none (hermetic replay queue-position at 1366x768, click on job 79)
- evidence_screenshot: ux/rounds/R-11/shots/review-pane-queued-79.png
- evidence_lines: pane text is "#79 scheduler-with-a-long-repository-name: cargo test …", "● live", "follow", "follow newest", "close" and no line of output
- regression_asset: ux/findings.spec.ts "UXF-025 queue-position: the pane of a queued job says queued, its place and its wait, and not \"live\"" and "UXF-025 call-mode: the pane of a job that waits for disk space names the reason"
- scenarios: 8 of 11 have queued jobs; on the card's path in 4 (queue-position, queue-levels, call-mode, upgrade-pending)
- cost: S

The daemon keeps the output stream of a queued job open until the job ends, so the pane's state is
"live" and its body is blank. A user who clicks the job an agent asked about reads "live" in red
over nothing: neither that the job has not started, nor its place in the queue, nor why it waits.

One-line fix: for a queued job the pane says "queued, #4 of 6, waiting 31s" (and the wait reason)
until the first output arrives.

Fix (polish after R-11): the pane states the job's state in words. A queued job reads "queued, #4 of 6,
waiting 31s" with the line "Not started yet: the output appears here when the job gets its slots" and
its wait reason; a running one "running, output live"; an ended one "failed (exit 101)" or "ok (exit 0)".
