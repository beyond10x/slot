# UXF-022: By keyboard an opened output is 15 tabs from its job, and Escape does not close it

- id: UXF-022
- title: By keyboard an opened output is 15 tabs from its job, and Escape does not close it
- class: affordance
- severity: S2
- metric: screen_only.keyboard_tabs (the bot stops counting at the job; tabs from the job to the pane are not counted yet)
- status: closed
- found_by: heuristic review, R-11 (heuristic 16, focus follows the work)
- evidence_recording: none (hermetic replay busy-failed at 1366x768, Enter on "output of job 42")
- evidence_screenshot: ux/rounds/R-11/shots/review-pane-42-keyboard.png
- evidence_lines: after Enter the focus stays on "output of job 42"; 15 Tab presses reach the pane's first control, 17 reach "close" (ux/rounds/R-11/shots/review-pane-42-focus-close.png); Escape leaves the pane open
- regression_asset: ux/findings.spec.ts "UXF-022 busy-failed: Enter on a job moves the focus into its output pane, Escape closes it and the focus is on the job again"
- scenarios: 3 of 11 (busy-failed, pool-share, crowded)
- cost: S

The pane is the last element of the page, so the keyboard walks every job button and the six usage
radios between the opener and the output. To scroll the error text or close the pane the user of
busy-failed presses Tab 15 to 17 times; the count grows with the rows below the opener (the
crowded scenario has 40 finished jobs). The pointer needs one click for either.

One-line fix: Escape closes the pane and returns focus to the opener, and one key or a link next
to the job number moves focus into the output.

Fix (R-11): a job button pressed by the keyboard moves the focus to the pane's "close" (the output is one
Tab further); Escape closes the pane from anywhere and puts the focus back on the control that opened
it, as does "close" pressed by the keyboard. A pointer click leaves the focus where it was.
